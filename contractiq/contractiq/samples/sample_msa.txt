MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into as of March 1, 2026 between Acme Legal Tech Pvt Ltd ("Provider") and Northwind Research Labs ("Client").

1. Term. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for twenty-four (24) months unless terminated earlier.

2. Termination. Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by providing thirty (30) days prior written notice to the other party. Either party may terminate immediately upon a material breach that remains uncured for fifteen (15) days after notice.

3. Fees. Client shall pay Provider a monthly retainer of INR 250,000. Invoices are due Net 30. Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

4. Confidentiality. Each party shall protect Confidential Information using reasonable care and for a period of three (3) years after termination.

5. Limitation of Liability. Except for confidentiality breaches, neither party's aggregate liability shall exceed fees paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages.

6. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Disputes shall be resolved by arbitration in Mumbai.
