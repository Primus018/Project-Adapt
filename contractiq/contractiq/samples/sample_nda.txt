NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is effective as of 15 January 2026 between Horizon Analytics LLP ("Disclosing Party") and Cascade Softworks Pvt Ltd ("Receiving Party").

1. Purpose. The parties wish to explore a potential collaboration on an NLP research platform and may share Confidential Information.

2. Definition. "Confidential Information" means all non-public technical, business, and financial information disclosed in writing or orally and marked confidential, including model weights, evaluation scripts, and customer lists.

3. Obligations. Receiving Party shall (a) use Confidential Information only for the Purpose, (b) restrict access to employees with a need to know, and (c) protect it with at least the same care used for its own confidential data, and no less than reasonable care.

4. Exclusions. Obligations do not apply to information that is publicly available, independently developed, or rightfully received from a third party without restriction.

5. Term. Confidentiality obligations survive for five (5) years from disclosure. Trade secrets remain protected for so long as they qualify as trade secrets.

6. Return. Upon written request, Receiving Party shall return or securely destroy Confidential Information within fourteen (14) days and certify destruction.

7. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Exclusive jurisdiction lies with the courts of Bengaluru.
